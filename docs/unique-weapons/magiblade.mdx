### Unique Effect: Magisonic

The Magiblade occasionally detects and repels approaching enemies.

### Hold Right Click

After charging, summon a Warden head that orbits you for 15.0 seconds and periodically fires sonic waves at nearby enemies. Using the ability again refreshes its duration.
