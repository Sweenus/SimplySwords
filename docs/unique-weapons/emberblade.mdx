```
Weapon Type: 1H Longsword
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 9.0
Attack Speed: 1.6
```

### Unique Effect:  
◇ Ember Ire


### Right Click:  
◇ Channel energy into the blade. Upon release, send forth fiery hot shrapnel at the nearest target in front of you, dealing increased damage the longer the channel.  
  
◇ Releasing the channel within the last second allows you to fire an additional piece of shrapnel.  

◇ When releasing shrapnel, you have a chance relative to your channel time to gain Speed, Haste, and Strength.
