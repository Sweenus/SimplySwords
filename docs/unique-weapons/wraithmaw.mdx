### Unique Effect: Spectral Downpour

When The Devourer's mass consumes a dropped Wraithfang, it corrupts the weapon into Wraithmaw.

When collected, spectral cutlasses float above you, seeking targets when you swing your weapon. Collect up to %s cutlasses for %ss.

### On Right Click

Send a barrage of spectral cutlasses to rain down upon the targeted area. Each cutlass performs a full weapon hit, embeds where it lands, and leaves behind Gloam.

Tendrils emerge from Gloam, grasping at enemies to slow and root them. Enemies that die within it feed the Gloam and cause it to spread.
