###Unique Effect:  
◇ Soul Rend: Chance on hit to inflict stacking weakening effects.


###Right Click:  
◇ Consume all stacks from surrounding foes, damaging them and healing you relative to the stacks consumed.  

◇ Attack combo varies depending on if the weapon is dual wielded or not.
