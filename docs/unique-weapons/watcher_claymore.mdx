
### Unique Effect: The Unblinking Hunt

Your weapon hits build up to 5 Dread on targets, summoning a bat for each stack.

### On Right Click

A stream of bats repeatedly tear into the enemy before delivering a final attack based on the targets missing health.

At maximum Dread, enemies left below 25% health are executed.

Killing the target resets the cooldown.
