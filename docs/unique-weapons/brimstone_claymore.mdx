```
Weapon Type: Claymore
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 12
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.2
Implicit: 5–15% chance to deflect incoming damage
```

### Unique Effect: Brimstone

Chance on hit to spew forth explosive brimstone.

### On Right Click

Summon a Brimstone Claymore above the target that pulses area damage before plunging into the ground.
