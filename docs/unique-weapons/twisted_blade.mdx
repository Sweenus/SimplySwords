```
Weapon Type: Claymore
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 6
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.4
Implicit: 5–15% chance to deflect incoming damage
```

### Unique Effect: Ferocity

Chance on hit to gain Ferocity, increasing attack speed and stacking up to 15 times.

While you have Ferocity, your hits can trigger Crescendo strikes that damage and knock back nearby enemies.

### On Right Click

Consume all Ferocity to empower your next successful hit with Grand Crescendo.
