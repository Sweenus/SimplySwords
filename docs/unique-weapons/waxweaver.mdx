### Unique Effect: Waxweave

Attacking ignited enemies grants you Strength and Haste.

Encase an enemy in wax, preventing it from moving or attacking, and causing nearby enemies to attack it.
The wax prison detonates when it expires or its victim dies.

When you would die, you are instead imbued with regenerative wax, healing you to full and granting resistance for a short time.

This effect can only occur once every 60s. Mainhand only.
