### Unique Effect: Bramblethorn

Chance on hit to release a swarm of toxic spores that orbit you and inflict increasingly devastating effects on nearby enemies.

The spores will remain active as long as they have a target within range, and can remain active at reduced effectiveness even when swapping weapons.
