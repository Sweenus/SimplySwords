```
Weapon Type: 1H Spear
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 10
Attack Speed: 1.9
```

### Unique Effect:  
◇ Magislam: Has innate Loyalty III


◇ When attacking enemies, you have a chance to deal additional magic damage.


### Right Click:  
◇ Throw the Magispear, and perform a forward leap, followed by a slam attack, damaging nearby enemies.  

◇ You are immune while leaping.
