```
Weapon Type: Spear
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 10
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.5
Implicit: 15–35% chance to ignore armour
```

### Unique Effect: Magislam

Has innate Loyalty III

Chance on hit to deal additional magic damage.

### On Right Click

Throw the Magispear and perform a forward leap, followed by a slam attack, damaging nearby enemies.

You are immune while leaping.
