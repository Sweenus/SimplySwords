### Unique Effect: Smoulder

Your attacks inflict Smouldering.

Attacking enemies inflicted with Smouldering causes you to deal an additional 0.2 damage per stack.

### On Right Click

Evade backwards while cauterising your wounds, healing for 15% of Max HP.
