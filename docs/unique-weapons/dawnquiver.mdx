### Unique Effect: Seraph's Draw

A lesser bow periodically forms beside you and lets loose an arrow of light at the most distant enemy you can see.
Successful hits have a chance to grant one stack of **Dawn Chorus**, up to a maximum of 3.

### On Right Click

Hold to draw a great bow of light, firing an arrow based on your release timing and Dawn Chorus charges:

1. **Dawn Volley:** Spend one Chorus to loose a second arrow.
2. **Sunlance:** Spend two Chorus to fire through up to four enemies, losing some damage after each target.
3. **Seraphic Chorus:** Spend three Chorus to loose a full-powered arrow, then summon three lesser bows around its impact that fire inward in sequence.
