### Unique Effect: Crimson Revelry

Hits inflict Bleed. Striking an enemy already at maximum Bleed consumes its Bleed in a damaging blood burst, inflicts Bleed on nearby enemies, and grants one stack of Blood Frenzy (up to five).

Blood bursts, Blood Plague spreads, and blood waves stain the ground for 30 seconds. Blood-stained ground slows enemies and restores half a heart every 2 seconds while you stand upon it.

### On Right Click

Consume every stack of Blood Frenzy to unleash an ability depending on the number consumed:

1. **Crimson Wake:** Send forth a blood wave that pushes enemies and inflicts Blood Plague. Enemies with Blood Plague spread Bleed to nearby enemies when struck.
2. **Blood-Curdling Cry:** Scream to gain 10% attack speed for each nearby bleeding enemy, up to 60%, for 10 seconds.
3. **Scarlet Judgment:** Conjure a spectral Bloodwake above each nearby bleeding enemy. After hovering briefly, each blade plunges for a full weapon hit plus 20% damage per Bleed stack on its target.
4. **Carrion Host:** Release a swarm of blood flies for 10 seconds. Their repeated diving attacks inflict Bleed.
5. **Red Tide:** For 5 seconds, release one blood wave per second from your current position. Each wave seeks a nearby enemy, or travels in a random direction when no target is nearby, pushing enemies and inflicting Bleed and Blood Plague. Activating Red Tide again while it is active consumes your Blood Frenzy and adds another 5 seconds to its duration.
