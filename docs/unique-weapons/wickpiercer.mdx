```
Attack Speed: 1.9
```

###Unique Effect:  
◇ Flicker Fury: When you would die, you are instead imbued with regenerative wax, healing you to full and granting resistance for a short time.  


◇ This effect can only occur once every Xs. Mainhand only.


###Right Click:  
◇ Instill your mind with fury, gaining a significant bonus to attack speed and allowing each strike of Wickpiercer to hit twice for Xs.
