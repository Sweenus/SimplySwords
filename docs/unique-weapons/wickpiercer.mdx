```
Weapon Type: Spear
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 10
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.9
Implicit: 15–35% chance to ignore armour
```

### Unique Effect: Flicker Fury

Has innate Loyalty III

When you would die, you are instead imbued with regenerative wax, healing you to full and granting resistance for a short time.

### On Right Click

Throw Wickpiercer, gaining frenzy stacks that grant attackspeed and allow you to hit twice for 4s.

Max 5 stacks. Lost on hit.
