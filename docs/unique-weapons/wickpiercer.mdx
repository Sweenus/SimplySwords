```
Weapon Type: 1H Spear
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 10
Attack Speed: 1.9
```

### Unique Effect:  
◇ Flicker Fury:  Has innate Loyalty III
  

◇ When you would die, you are instead imbued with regenerative wax, healing you to full and granting resistance for a short time.


◇ This effect can only occur once every Xs. Mainhand only.


### Right Click:  
◇ Instill your mind with fury, throwing Wickpiercer and gaining stacks of Frenzy that grant a significant bonus to attack speed and allow each strike of Wickpiercer to hit twice for Xs.  

◇ You can have a maximum of 5 stacks of Frenzy, and 1 stack is removed everytime you strike a target.
