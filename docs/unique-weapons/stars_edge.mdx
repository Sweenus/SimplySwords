```
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 9
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 2.0
Implicit: 5–25% chance to gain attack speed on hit
```

### Unique Effect: Astral Reprise

During the day, your attacks inflict 0.32 additional damage.

At night, your attacks gain lifesteal.

### On Right Click

Dash forward, and leave a constellation trail for a short duration.

The constellation trail damages enemies that touch it, and explodes each star for area damage when it expires.
