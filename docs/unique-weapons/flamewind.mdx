```
Weapon Type: Glaive
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 9
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.4
Implicit: 10–60% chance to inflict Bleed
```

### Unique Effect: Emberstorm

When a flame seed detonates, gain Haste.

### On Right Click

Plant a seed of flame within the nearest enemy, dealing damage over time.

Upon expiring, the seed detonates, dealing area damage and spreading the effect to nearby enemies.

Spreads up to 6 times.
