```
Weapon Type: Spear
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 10
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.9
Implicit: 15–35% chance to ignore armour
```

### Unique Effect: Phantom Phalanx

When The Devourer's mass consumes a dropped Wickpiercer, it corrupts the weapon into Gloampiercer.

Swinging toward an enemy between 3 and 12 blocks away summons an airborne shadow clone that hurls an explosive Gloampiercer at them.

### On Right Click

Rise into the air and summon a phalanx of shadow clones to bombard the targeted area. Every spear performs a full weapon hit and stains the terrain with slowing gloam.

Missed spears remain embedded for 5 seconds, exploding early when approached by an enemy.
