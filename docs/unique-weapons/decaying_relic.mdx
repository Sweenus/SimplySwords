### Progression

A faint power seems to emanate from within.

The Decaying Relic reacts to the Deep Dark and preserves its components and awakening level when it changes form:

- Taking a Warden sonic boom transforms it into **Magiscythe**.
- Standing on a Sculk Sensor gives it a chance to become **Magiblade**.
- Experiencing Darkness gives it a rarer chance to become **Magispear**.

Try keeping this in your inventory while exploring the deepest, darkest depths of the Overworld.
