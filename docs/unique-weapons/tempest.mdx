### Unique Effect: Vortex

Your attacks attach elemental energy to the target, causing damage over time and element specific debuffs.

This effect stacks.

### On Right Click

Return all nearby elements to yourself, forming a vortex of elemental energy around you, damaging nearby enemies and growing in size.
