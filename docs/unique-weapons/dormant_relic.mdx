```
Weapon Type: Longsword
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 9
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.6
Implicit: 5–15% chance to deflect incoming damage
```

### Progression

At level 4 in the Runic Forge, the Dormant Relic becomes a **Righteous Relic** during daytime in the Overworld or a **Tainted Relic** at night.

At level 8, Righteous Relic becomes **Sunfire**, while Tainted Relic becomes **Harbinger**.

A faint power seems to emanate from within.
