```
Weapon Type: 1H Axe
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 10
Attack Speed: 1.9
```

### Unique Effect:  
◇ Molten Roar: Chance on hit to ignite your enemy or yourself. If low HP, gain regeneration instead.  

◇ Gain strength and speed relative to missing HP.

### Right Click:  
◇ Release a powerful roar, knocking back and igniting nearby enemies.  
  
◇ Gain resistance and onslaught with a duration relative to enemies hit.
