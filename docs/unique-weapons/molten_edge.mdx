```
Weapon Type: Greataxe
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 10
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.9
Implicit: 10–60% chance to inflict Bleed
```

### Unique Effect: Furnace Heart

Dealing or receiving damage builds Heat, increasing damage dealt and received.

At maximum Heat, deal 50% more damage, take 100% more damage, and become ignited.

Heat is lost when Molten Edge is no longer wielded.

### On Right Click

Vent the stored heat, creating a molten shockwave. While venting, weapon swings release magma ruptures.
