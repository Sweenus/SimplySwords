### Unique Effect:  
◇ Frost Fury: Chance on hit to encase nearby enemies in ice, causing them to take no damage.  

◇ After Xs the ice shatters causing X damage.


### Right Click:  
◇ Encase yourself in ice for Xs, rendering you immune and granting you regeneration.
