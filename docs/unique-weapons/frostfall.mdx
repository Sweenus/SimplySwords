```
Weapon Type: 1H Hammer
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 11
Attack Speed: 1.5
```

### Unique Effect:  
◇ Frost Fury: Has innate Loyalty III


### Right Click:  
◇ Throw Frostfall forward.  

◇ Deal increased damage the longer Frostfall spends airborne.  

◇ Landing a direct hit at the right angle will deal bonus damage.  

◇ After landing, Frostfall pulses 5 times, damaging, slowing, and pulling enemies.  

◇ When near another Frostfall, each pulse has a chance to repeat itself.

◇ Frostfall can be thrown from the offhand or mainhand. (Try dual wielding it!)
