```
Implicit: 5–15% chance to strike twice
```

### Unique Effect: The Unblinking Hunt

Your weapon hits build up to 5 Dread on targets, summoning a bat for each stack.

### On Right Click

Release your bats through nearby enemies afflicted with Dread. Strike and siphon life for every Dread stack consumed.
