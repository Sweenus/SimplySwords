### Unique Effect: Vanguard

Swinging toward an enemy between 4 and 16 blocks away has a chance to tear open a rift ahead of you
and loose a single spectral charger, which gallops down the target and hurls it aside.

### On Right Click

Split the air into a rank of five spectral chargers that surge forward together, performing a full
weapon hit and knocking back everything caught in their lanes.

Using the ability while sprinting causes you to mount the central charger.

Chargers climb terrain up to one block high, and gallop across the surface of open water so long as
you were not underwater when you called them.
