```
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 13
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.3
Implicit: 10–60% chance to inflict Bleed
```

### Unique Effect: Permafrost

While wielded, gain a frost aura that deals damage and slows enemies within 4 blocks.

### On Right Click

Ice comets crash down around you for a duration, dealing area damage where they land.
