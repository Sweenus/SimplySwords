### Unique Effect:  
◇ Fatal Flicker: Chance on hit to refresh ability cooldown.


### Right Click:  
◇ If within 5 blocks of a soulless target, return their soul, leap forward, and gain invisibility.

◇ Perform a swift forward dash, gaining resistance and absorption, and inflicting enemies in your path with Echo.

◇ For every target hit by the dash, the amount of Echo you inflict is increased.
