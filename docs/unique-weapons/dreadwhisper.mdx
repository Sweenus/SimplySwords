```
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 2.0
Implicit: 5–15% chance to deal double damage
```

### Unique Effect: Shadow Rend

When The Devourer's mass consumes a dropped Whisperwind, it corrupts the weapon into Dreadwhisper.

Targets struck by Shadow Rend suffer a Corrupted Wound. Striking a wounded target with Dreadwhisper guarantees a critical hit and ruptures the wound.

### On Right Click

Become a moving wall of dark energy and surge through enemies while briefly immune to damage.

Each enemy in the path takes a weapon hit once, suffers a Corrupted Wound, and heals you for a portion of the health removed. The surge leaves Gloam across its full width.

Tendrils emerge from Gloam, grasping at enemies to slow and root them. Enemies that die within it feed the Gloam and cause it to spread.
