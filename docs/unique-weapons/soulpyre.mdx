### Unique Effect:  
◇ Soul Tether


### Right Click:  
◇ An expanding zone forms around you. Each pulse heals you and damages enemies.

◇ While active, you gain knockback resistance and take 50% reduced damage.

◇ Whenever an enemy dies to this effect, each subsequent pulse has increased damage.
