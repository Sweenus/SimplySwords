### Unique Effect: Soul Tether

### On Right Click

Cause the landscape around you to shift into the nether. Slaying enemies within this malformed landscape causes it to grow larger.

Defeated enemies grant you souls, which are periodically fired at nearby enemies.

While active, you gain knockback resistance and take 50% reduced damage.
