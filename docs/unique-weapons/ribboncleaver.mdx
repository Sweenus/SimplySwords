```
Weapon Type: Claymore
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 13
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 0.8
Implicit: 5–15% chance to deflect incoming damage
```

### Unique Effect: Ribbonwrath

Wielding Ribboncleaver causes you to move 5% slower, but take 15% less damage.

### On Right Click

Dash forward, shrugging off the next few incoming hits, gaining knockback resistance, and causing your next attack to deal +95% damage to a single target.
