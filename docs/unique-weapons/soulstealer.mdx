```
Weapon Type: 1H Dagger
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 6.0
Attack Speed: 2.5
```

###Unique Effect:  
◇ Soul Steal: Chance on hit to absorb your opponents soul, hastening you while slowing and marking your target for death.


###Right Click:  
◇ If within 5 blocks of a soulless target, return their soul, leap forward, and gain invisibility.

◇ If more than 5 blocks from a soulless target, blink to their location, returning their soul with force, blinding and damaging them.
