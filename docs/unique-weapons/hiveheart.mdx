###Unique Effect:  
◇ Hivemind: Release an angry bee on hit that stings your target before disappearing.  

◇ This effect can only occur once every Xs.


###Right Click:  

◇ Your bees focus on producing regenerative honey, healing you over time but placing the Hivemind ability on cooldown.
