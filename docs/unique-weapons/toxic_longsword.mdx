### Unique Effect: Death Knell

Weapon hits inflict the target with fever. At 5 fever, a plague bell tolls and damages nearby enemies.

The toll spreads fever and can cascade through up to 6 enemies.

Hits can convert positive effects into ailments, granting bonus fever and empowering the next toll.
