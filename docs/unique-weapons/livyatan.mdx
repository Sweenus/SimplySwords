```
Weapon Type: Greataxe
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 10
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.9
Implicit: 10–60% chance to inflict Bleed
```

### Unique Effect: Leviathan's Wake

### On Right Click

Throw Livyatan forward. Airtime and angle affect impact damage.

On return, Livyatan creates a wave that pulls enemies, and has a chance to strike them with lightning.

Has innate Loyalty III. Weapon swings release a damaging wave.
