Attack Damage: 10
Attack Speed: 1.9

### Unique Effect:  
◇ Frost Shatter: Chance on hit to encase nearby enemies in ice, causing them to take no damage.  

◇ After Xs the ice shatters causing X damage.

### Right Click:  
◇ Preemptively shatter the ice encasing your enemies, dealing more damage the earlier you shatter.
