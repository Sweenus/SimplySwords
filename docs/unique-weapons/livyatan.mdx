Weapon Type: 1H Axe
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 10
Attack Speed: 1.9

### Unique Effect:  
◇ Jötunn's Wrath: Has innate Loyalty III

### Right Click:  
◇ Throw Livyatan forward.

◇ Deal increased damage the longer Livyatan spends airborne.  

◇ Landing a direct hit at the right angle will deal bonus damage.  

◇ Livyatan damages and slows enemies on its return to you.

◇ Livyatan can be thrown from the offhand or mainhand. (Try dual wielding it!)
