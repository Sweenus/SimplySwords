### Unique Effect: Stygian Stride

When The Devourer's mass consumes a dropped Soulrender, it corrupts the weapon into Soulstalker.

Abyssal tendrils periodically emerge from your back and pierce nearby enemies.

### On Right Click

Rise upon six abyssal limbs to stride across the terrain. Charge jump to perform a directional leap.

While in-use, footsteps damage nearby enemies, weapon swings release shadow cleaves, and every tentacle impact leaves Gloam behind.

Tendrils emerge from Gloam, grasping at enemies to slow and root them. Enemies that die within it feed the Gloam and cause it to spread.

Sneak to end Stygian Stride early.
