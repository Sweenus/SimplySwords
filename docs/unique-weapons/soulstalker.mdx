### Unique Effect: Stygian Stride

When The Devourer's mass consumes a dropped Soulrender, it corrupts the weapon into Soulstalker.

Periodically, an abyssal tendril lashes from your back to pierce a nearby enemy with a full weapon hit, staining the ground beneath them with slowing gloam.

### On Right Click

Rise upon six abyssal limbs for 15 seconds, striding swiftly over uneven terrain and crawling across walls and ceilings. While in-use, footsteps damage nearby enemies, while steps taken on the ground leave behind slowing gloam.

Charge jump to perform a directional leap. Weapon swings release growing shadow cleaves that travel up to 10 blocks and perform full weapon hits.

Sneak to end Stygian Stride early.
