```
Weapon Type: 2H Hammer
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 14
Attack Speed: 1.1
```

###Unique Effect:  
◇ Soul Meld: Chance on hit to absorb nearby souls, toughening and slowing the attack speed of the wielder.


###Right Click:  
◇ Release the souls, transferring the attack speed debuff to nearby foes and regenerating the wielder.
