```
Weapon Type: Greathammer
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 14
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.1
Implicit: Sunders 2–10% armour per hit
```

### Unique Effect: Soul Meld

Two soul lanterns orbit you, damaging enemies they touch. Weapon and lantern hits increase their orbit speed.

### On Right Click

Summon two additional soul lanterns for a short duration.
