```
Weapon Type: Longsword
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 9
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.6
Implicit: 5–15% chance to deflect incoming damage
```

### Unique Effect: Shock Deflect

On hit, spend a Storm Charge to fire chain lightning between nearby enemies.

### On Right Click

Focus energy to a single point of your blade, becoming briefly immune to incoming damage.

Perfectly timing this ability triggers a counterattack and grants Storm Charges.
