### Progression

The Lichblade changes form in the Runic Forge as tablets are added:

- **Level 0–3:** Slumbering Lichblade — Soul Anguish I.
- **Level 4–7:** Waking Lichblade — Soul Anguish II.
- **Level 8:** Awakened Lichblade — Soul Anguish III.

### Unique Effect: Soul Anguish I–III

Tortured souls radiate from you, damaging any nearby enemies.

### Hold Right Click

Command your souls to attack a distant target, dealing rapid damage. Chance to siphon health.

After the attack subsides, your souls return to you and, when reaching you, grant you absorption.

*The returning-soul effect is unlocked at Soul Anguish III.*
