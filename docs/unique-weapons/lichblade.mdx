```
Weapon Type: 2H Claymore
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 13
Attack Speed: 0.9
Can be upgraded via crafting.
```

###Unique Effect:  
◇ Soul Anguish: Tortured souls radiate from you, damaging any nearby enemies.


###Right Click:  
◇ Command your souls to attack a distant target, dealing rapid damage. Chance to siphon health.

◇ After the attack subsides, your souls return to you and, when reaching you, grant you absorption.
