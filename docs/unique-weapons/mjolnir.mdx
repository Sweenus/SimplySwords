```
Implicit: Sunders 2–6% armour per hit
```

### Unique Effect: Storm

Weapon hits cause enemies to become Conductive.

### On Right Click

Summon a mobile storm that repeatedly strikes nearby enemies before ending in a powerful thunderclap. Conductive enemies release a smaller thunderclap when struck.
