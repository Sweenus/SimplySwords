# What are Unique weapons?

---

Unique weapons are powerful weapons that possess unique abilities and deal slightly more damage than their Netherite counterparts.

---

# Where can I find Unique weapons?

---

###Chest Loot
Unique weapons can be found in any chest throughout your adventures, including chests from most modded structures. The chance to find a unique weapon is very low.
###Monster Loot
By default, Unique weapons can also be obtained by defeating the Wither & Ender Dragon at a 5% and 50% chance respectively.
###Crafting
Unique weapons cannot be crafted, however, some Unique weapons can be upgraded via crafting.

---

# How do I repair Unique weapons?

---

Most Unique weapons can be repaired using Runic Tablets, however, there are a few exceptions to this - take a look at the wiki entry for your Unique weapon to find its repair ingredient.
