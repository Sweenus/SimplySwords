```
Weapon Type: Scythe
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 10
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.6
Implicit: 5–15% chance to execute low-health enemies
```

### Unique Effect: Magistorm

Attacking enemies while Magistorm is active grants a chance to repair an equipped item.

### On Right Click

A localised magical storm manifests above you, dealing damage to nearby enemies. Each time the storm strikes, it has a chance to refresh its duration and increases its severity.
