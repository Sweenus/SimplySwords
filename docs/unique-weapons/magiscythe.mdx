###Unique Effect:  
◇ Magistorm: Attacking enemies while Magistorm is active grants a chance to repair an equipped item.


###Right Click:  
◇ A localised magical storm manifests above you, dealing damage to nearby enemies.

◇ Each time the storm strikes, it has a chance to refresh its duration and increases its severity.
