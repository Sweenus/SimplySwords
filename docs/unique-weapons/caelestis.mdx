```
Weapon Type: 2H Claymore
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 12.0
Attack Speed: 1.1
```

###Unique Effect:  
◇ Astral Shift: While wielded, grants a chance to completely avoid incoming damage.


###Right Click:  
◇ Briefly step into the astral plane, becoming immune to damage for Xs.

◇ Upon leaving the astral plane, release a violent explosion, dealing area damage relative to the damage negated while in the astral plane.
