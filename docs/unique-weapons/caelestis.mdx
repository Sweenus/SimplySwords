```
Weapon Type: Claymore
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Fully Awakened Attack Damage: 12
Fully Awakened Attack Speed: 1.1
Implicit: 5–15% chance to deflect incoming damage
```

### Unique Effect: Astral Breach

Tear open a breach into another dimension, summoning corrupted creatures that fight for you.

### On Right Click

The breach lasts 45s and expands to a 20 block radius.

It collapses over its final 5s, pulling the summons back to their dimension. Each breach has a 5% chance to release an Unbound creature that hunts you.
