###Unique Effect:  
◇ Volcanic Fury: Chance on hit to ignite your target and send them flying.


###Right Click:  
◇ Channel your fury, exchanging health to gain resistance, whilst causing tremors that pull enemies towards you.

◇ Stop channelling to unleash your fury, launching, igniting, and dealing more damage the longer the channel.
