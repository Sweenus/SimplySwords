### Unique Effect: Chains of the Furnace

Weapon hits can brand and ignite enemies, making them easier to bind with furnace chains.

### On Right Click

Bind nearby enemies with molten chains. Striking bound enemies echoes fire damage to other bound enemies.

As the chains are stretched, they build pressure. Attacking bound enemies also builds pressure.

At maximum pressure, your next strike shatters every remaining chain, dealing area damage.
