Simply Swords expands Minecraft combat with new weapon types, rolled weapon implicits, Runic powers, socketable gems, and powerful Unique weapons.

## Where should I start?

- **Weapon Types** explains the standard weapon families, their recipes, and their built-in implicits.
- **Awakening & Runic Forge** explains how Unique weapons recover their full power.
- **Gem Socketing**, **Runic Powers**, and **Nether Powers** cover weapon customisation.
- **Unique Weapons** lists every built-in Unique and its current ability.

Most values shown in this guide are defaults. Modpack and server configurations can change weapon statistics, loot chances, ability values, and enabled systems.

Hold **Alt** while viewing a weapon tooltip to reveal extended information such as implicit ranges, sockets, and awakening details.
