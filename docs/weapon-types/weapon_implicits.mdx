---
title: Weapon Implicits
icon: simplyswords:runic_longsword
---

An **Implicit** is a permanent bonus determined by a weapon's type. Standard, Runic, and Unique Simply Swords weapons can all roll one. Hold **Alt** over the weapon to see the roll's full range.

| Weapon type | Implicit | Default range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rapier, Spear | Chance to ignore armour | 15–35% |
| Cutlass | Chance to plunder extra loot on hit | 1–3% |
| Glaive, Greataxe, Halberd | Chance to inflict Bleed | 10–60% |
| Sai, Dagger | Increased backstab damage | 20–40% |
| Claymore, Longsword | Chance to deflect incoming damage | 5–15% |
| Greathammer | Armour sundered per hit | 2–10% |
| Hammer | Armour sundered per hit | 2–6% |
| Katana | Chance to deal double damage | 5–15% |
| Chakram, Twinblade | Chance to gain attack speed on hit | 5–25% |
| Scythe | Chance to execute a low-health enemy | 5–15% |
| Warglaive | Chance to strike twice | 5–15% |

Addon weapons can also receive implicits when they use a recognised item name or the appropriate Simply Swords implicit item tag. Implicits may be disabled globally in the configuration.
