##What are Runic Powers?
Runic powers provide the player with passive or active abilities. They can come in the form of Runefused gems which can be socketed into unique weapons, or etched into Netherite weapons, turning them into Runic weapons.

##What are Runic Weapons?
Runic weapons are created by etching runes onto Netherite weapons. Each Runic weapon will be etched with a Runic power that must be identified.

##How do I create a Runic Weapon?
Simply combine a Runic Tablet, a Netherite Weapon, and a diamond in a smithing table. Once created, left click the item in your inventory to identify the Runic power.

##How do I repair a Runic Weapon?
Runic weapons can be repaired with Netherite Ingots.

##How do I obtain a Runic Tablet?
Runic Tablets have a low chance to be found in any chest, including chests found in modded structures.

##How do I re-roll a Runic power?
Runic weapons can have their ability re-rolled by combining a Runic Tablet, the Runic Weapon, and a diamond in a smithing table. (Do not shift-click the item from the smithing table, or it will not re-roll the ability).

##What is the 'Greater' prefix on my Runic power?
Some (not all) Runic powers can occasionally roll with the 'Greater' prefix in the item tooltip. Greater Runic powers are enhanced versions of their ordinary counterparts.
