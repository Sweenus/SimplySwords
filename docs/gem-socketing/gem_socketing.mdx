Unique weapons in Simply Swords have a chance to contain Netherfused & Runefused gem sockets that can be socketed with a matching gem to add additional abilities to the weapon.

##How do I know if my weapon has gem sockets?
While viewing the weapon tooltip, hold ALT. If any sockets are present on the weapon they will appear in the tooltip.


##Why doesn't my weapon have gem sockets?
Not all unique weapons will have gem sockets. When a unique weapon is generated in the world, it has a 50% chance to receive a Rune and/or Nether gem socket. See Unique Weapons for a list of compatible weapons.


##How do I obtain/craft a gem?
Gems can be crafted by combining a few expensive ingredients in a smithing table:

◆ Netherfused gem: Runic Tablet + Empowered Remnant + Netherite Ingot.
◆ Runefused gem: Runic Tablet + Empowered Remnant + Crying Obsidian.
An Empowered Remnant can be obtained by smelting down any unique weapon.


##How do I socket a Runefused/Netherfused gem?
To socket a gem, you just need to anchor the gem to the mouse by clicking on it in your inventory, then click on a unique weapon with a matching gem socket. If the gem isn't socketing, you might not have a matching socket available on the weapon.


##Can I remove/replace a socketed gem?
Once socketed, gems cannot be removed. Be sure to choose carefully.
