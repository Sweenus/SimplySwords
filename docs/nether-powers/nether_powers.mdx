##What are Nether Powers?
Nether powers provide the player with passive or active abilities. They come in the form of Netherfused gems which can be socketed into unique weapons.

##How do I re-roll a Nether power?
If your Netherfused gem has an ability you don't want, you can smelt the gem back down to get back some of your materials.
