# Simply Swords

Simply Swords expands Minecraft combat with new weapon types, rolled weapon implicits, Runic powers, socketable gems, and powerful Unique weapons.

Use the documentation navigation to browse crafting recipes, progression systems, powers, and the complete built-in Unique weapon catalogue. Unique weapons found as loot begin unawakened by default and can be restored with Runic Tablets at a Runic Forge.

Most values in this guide are defaults and may be changed by a modpack or server configuration. Hold **Alt** while viewing a weapon tooltip to reveal additional weapon information.
